Common questions

Questions clients ask us.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Yes. Many of our clients are SMEs, startups and sole proprietors. We scale the service to your size — from filing a single monthly VAT return to running your full accounts, payroll and tax.

My books are behind. Can you help us catch up?

Yes. We start with a clean-up: reconciling bank accounts, recovering missing records and bringing ledgers up to date. Once you’re current, we keep them that way with monthly bookkeeping so you never fall behind again.

Does my business need eTIMS?

KRA requires businesses to issue electronic tax invoices through eTIMS, and expenses not supported by valid eTIMS invoices may not be allowed as deductions. We’ll check your situation, recommend the right eTIMS solution and set it up for you.

Can you run our payroll as well as our taxes?

Yes. We process payroll, issue payslips and P9 forms, and remit PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy alongside your other tax filings — so all your statutory obligations are handled in one place.

How are your fees structured?

Most ongoing services are billed as a fixed monthly fee agreed upfront. One-off projects such as eTIMS integration, SOP development or training are quoted per project. Contact us for our current pricing schedule.

How do you keep our financial data confidential?

Access to client records is limited to the team working on your account, and we handle personal and financial data in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. We never share your information without your consent.
